Privacy policy

The Eleven Seals · last updated 26 November 2026

The Eleven Seals is a free game made by one person, Patrick Safari. This page explains what the game does with information about you. It covers the game on the web, on itch.io and in the Android app (package com.elevenseals).

What the game keeps

  • Your saved games and settings (hero, progress, language, sound) are stored only on your own device, in the browser or app storage. They are never sent to me.
  • The game has no account and does not ask for your name, email, phone number or location. The hero name you choose stays on your device.

Ads (Android app)

In the Android app you can choose to watch a short ad to get gold and food. These ads come from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, your approximate location (from your IP address), and information about your device and how you use the ads. This is done by Google under its own policy: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. Ads are only shown when you press "Watch an ad". You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings › Google › Ads).

Buying gold (Android app)

Gold packs in the Android app are bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; the game never sees your card or payment details. The game only receives a note that the purchase was made, so it can give you the gold. See the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

Play with friends

If you start or join a party (Menu › Play with friends), your game connects directly to your friends' games through the free PeerJS service (0.peerjs.com) to set up the connection. Your hero's name, kingdom, place on the map and the short chat messages you send go to the players in your party. Nothing about the party is stored online, and it ends when the host leaves.

Children

The game is not made for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. The game itself collects none; ads are handled by Google AdMob as described above.

Your choices

  • Delete your saves at any time by clearing the app's data (Android Settings › Apps › The Eleven Seals › Storage) or the website's data in your browser.
  • Do not press "Watch an ad" if you do not want to see ads.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: write to me on the game's itch.io page or on Discord.
